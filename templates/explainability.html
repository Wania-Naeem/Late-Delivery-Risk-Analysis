Per-prediction explainability (SHAP)

Every prediction made on the Prediction page is explained individually using SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations). Rather than relying only on the global importances above, SHAP shows exactly which factors pushed that specific order's risk up or down — and only for the categories actually selected for that order, avoiding misleading "what if" comparisons.
